Schedule-Based Bible EPUB Creator

Select your preferred translation, provide your reading schedule CSV, customize your title, and generate your EPUB file. The resulting file should be compatible with your favorite ereader or an ereader app on your cell phone or computer web browser. When using your own CSV, please follow the link in the Reading Schedule section to see the correct formatting. Only copyright-free translations are available using this software. Translations are fetched using the HelloAO Free Use Bible API.
